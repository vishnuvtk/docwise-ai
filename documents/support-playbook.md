# Support Playbook

The support team targets a first response within one business day for standard internal requests.

High-severity launch blockers should be escalated to the operations lead within two hours of confirmation. If the blocker affects billing or contract obligations, the finance partner must also be notified the same day.

When a customer question falls outside the product documentation, the support team should admit that the current library is incomplete and route the issue to product operations instead of guessing.

Support notes should include the original question, the linked document sources, and the final response that was sent back to the requester.
